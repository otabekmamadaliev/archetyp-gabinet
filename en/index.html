Questions

Common questions

Do you take national health patients?

No. The practice is entirely private — we hold no contract with the national health fund. We say so up front so nobody makes the journey for nothing.

I am afraid of the dentist. What then?

Tell us when you book. The first visit can be limited to a conversation and a look, with no treatment at all. For many people that is enough to come back for a second.

What does root canal treatment cost?

Between 700 and 1400 zł, depending on how many canals the tooth has. We give the exact figure after the X-ray, before treatment begins.

Can I pay in instalments?

Where treatment is spread over several appointments, you pay per appointment. For larger prosthetic plans we agree a schedule individually.

What if it hurts at the weekend?

We see patients until 13:00 on Saturdays. Outside our hours the nearest emergency dental service is at the hospital — the number is on our voicemail.

From what age do you see children?

From three years old. A child’s first visit is always familiarisation — we look around, count teeth, and force nothing.

About

One person per hour

We run the practice so that nobody sits in the waiting room half an hour past their time. Appointments do not overlap, so if something takes longer it does not cost the next patient their slot.

We do not sell packages and we do not propose treatment you do not need. If a tooth can be saved, we say so. If it cannot, we say that too.
